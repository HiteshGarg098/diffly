diffly

Free online diff checker & text compare tool. Paste two texts, find every difference, merge changes — with syntax highlighting for 30+ languages. 100% private: your text never leaves your browser.

  • ↔️ Split & unified diff views
  • 🔀 Cherry-pick or take-all merge
  • 🎨 Auto-detect 30+ languages
  • 📋 Export to .patch or HTML report
  • 🔗 Share via link (text stays in the URL fragment, never sent to a server)
  • 🔒 No server, no tracking, no sign-up
  • ⚡ 10k-line diffs in ~1 second
